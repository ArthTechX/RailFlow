READY FOR THE WALKTHROUGH

Take RailFlow for a spin.

A complete booking story, in a few clicks.

01

Find your train

Search Delhi to Mumbai for tomorrow. Switch travel classes to compare sample fares and seats.

02

Bring your people

Add up to six passengers. On the first train, choose 2A and add three people to demonstrate two allocated seats and one waitlisted passenger.

03

Confirm the journey

Choose a successful or failed mock payment. A failed payment releases the seat hold; a successful one creates a demo ticket.

04

Change your plans

Open My trips to view a ticket or cancel a booking. Paid tickets receive an instant simulated refund.

Everything here is a simulation.

No Google login, Docker, or payment credentials are needed. This prototype uses browser storage and does not demonstrate real distributed locking.
